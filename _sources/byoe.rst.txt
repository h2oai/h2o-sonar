What are BYOE recipes?
----------------------
Techniques and methodologies used by H2O Sonar for model interpretation can
be **extended** with **recipes**. **Bring your own explainer (BYOE)** feature allows
you to use your explainers in combination with or in place of H2O Sonar built-in
explainers (all H2O Sonar explainers are in fact BYOEs). This lets you extend
the capabilities of H2O Sonar explainers and out-of-the-box interpretation techniques.
