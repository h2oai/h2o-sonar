Dataset: kaggle_llm_science_exam_*.json
Source: Kaggle LLM Science Exam Competition
License: Kaggle Competition Dataset License

Description:
Dataset from the Kaggle LLM Science Exam competition where participants developed
models to answer difficult science questions. The questions were generated by GPT-3.5
based on Wikipedia articles covering various scientific topics.

Original Source: Kaggle Competition
URL: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/kaggle-llm-science-exam
Competition Period: 2023
Platform: Kaggle
