Description:
NIST AI 600-1 is the "Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative
Artificial Intelligence Profile" published by the National Institute of Standards and
Technology (NIST). This dataset contains questions and test scenarios derived from
NIST's guidance on managing risks associated with generative AI systems.

Content Description:
The dataset contains test scenarios and evaluation prompts derived from NIST's
Generative AI Profile, which provides guidance on:
- Managing risks unique to generative AI systems
- Trustworthy AI principles applied to generative AI
- TEVV (Test, Evaluation, Verification, and Validation) for generative AI
- Governance structures for generative AI deployment
