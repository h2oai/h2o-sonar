Description:
SR 11-7 is a Supervision and Regulation Letter issued by the Board of Governors of
the Federal Reserve System titled "Guidance on Model Risk Management." This dataset
contains questions, test scenarios, and evaluation prompts derived from this guidance

Content Description:
The dataset contains test scenarios and evaluation prompts derived from SR 11-7,
which provides guidance on:
- Model risk management for financial institutions
- Model development, implementation, and use
- Model validation practices
- Governance, policies, and controls
- Roles and responsibilities for model risk management
- Documentation standards for models
- Ongoing monitoring and model review

This guidance is widely used by banks and financial institutions supervised by the
Federal Reserve System to establish sound model risk management frameworks.

Regulatory Context:
SR 11-7 is one of the most influential guidance documents for model risk management
in the financial services industry. It defines model risk as arising from:
1. Potential adverse consequences from decisions based on incorrect or misused model outputs
2. Potential adverse consequences from incorrect or misused models

Notes:
- SR 11-7 applies to all types of models used by financial institutions
- Emphasizes the importance of effective challenge in model validation
- Requires ongoing monitoring and periodic validation of models
- Has been widely adopted beyond Federal Reserve supervised institutions
- Complements other regulatory guidance (OCC 2011-12, FDIC FIL-22-2017)
