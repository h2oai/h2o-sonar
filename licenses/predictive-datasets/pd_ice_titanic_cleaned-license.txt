Dataset: Titanic - Machine Learning from Disaster
Source: Kaggle Competition
URL: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/titanic

License: Kaggle Competition License / Apache 2.0 (varies by version)

Description:
The Titanic dataset contains passenger information (age, sex, class, etc.) and survival
outcomes from the RMS Titanic disaster. The goal is to predict passenger survival. This
is one of the most popular introductory machine learning datasets.
