Dataset: Dry Bean Dataset
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository
DOI: 10.24432/C50S4B
URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/602/dry+bean+dataset

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Description:
Images of 13,611 grains of 7 different registered dry beans were taken with a high-resolution
camera. A total of 16 features; 12 dimensions and 4 shape forms were obtained from the grains.
This dataset is suitable for multiclass classification tasks.

Features:
- Area: Bean area (integer)
- Perimeter: Bean perimeter (continuous)
- MajorAxisLength: Length of major axis (continuous)
- MinorAxisLength: Length of minor axis (continuous)
- AspectRatio: Aspect ratio (continuous)
- Eccentricity: Eccentricity (continuous)
- ConvexArea: Convex area (integer)
- EquivDiameter: Equivalent diameter (continuous)
- Extent: Extent (continuous)
- Solidity: Solidity (continuous)
- Roundness: Roundness (continuous)
- Compactness: Compactness (continuous)
- ShapeFactor1: Shape factor 1 (continuous)
- ShapeFactor2: Shape factor 2 (continuous)
- ShapeFactor3: Shape factor 3 (continuous)
- ShapeFactor4: Shape factor 4 (continuous)
- Class: Bean variety (SEKER, BARBUNYA, BOMBAY, CALI, DERMASON, HOROZ, SIRA)

Dataset Statistics:
- Total Instances: 13,611
- Number of Features: 16 (all numeric)
- Number of Classes: 7
- Class Distribution:
  - DERMASON: 3,546 (26.1%)
  - SIRA: 2,636 (19.4%)
  - SEKER: 2,027 (14.9%)
  - HOROZ: 1,928 (14.2%)
  - CALI: 1,630 (12.0%)
  - BARBUNYA: 1,322 (9.7%)
  - BOMBAY: 522 (3.8%)
