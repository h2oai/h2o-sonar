Dataset: Bank Marketing Dataset (with synthesized dates)
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository - Bank Marketing Dataset
DOI: 10.24432/C5K306
URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Description:
The Bank Marketing dataset is related to direct marketing campaigns (phone calls) of a
Portuguese banking institution. The classification goal is to predict if the client will
subscribe to a term deposit (variable y).

This version includes a synthesized 'contact_date' column created from the original 'month'
and 'day_of_week' columns, representing the contact date in YYYY-MM-DD format. The dates
span from May 2008 to December 2010, matching the original data collection period.

Features (19 total):
- age: Client age (numeric)
- job: Type of job (categorical: admin, blue-collar, entrepreneur, etc.)
- marital: Marital status (categorical: divorced, married, single, unknown)
- education: Education level (categorical: basic, high school, professional, university, unknown)
- default: Has credit in default? (categorical: no, yes, unknown)
- balance: Average yearly balance in euros (numeric)
- housing: Has housing loan? (categorical: no, yes, unknown)
- loan: Has personal loan? (categorical: no, yes, unknown)
- contact: Contact communication type (categorical: cellular, telephone)
- day_of_week: Last contact day of week (numeric: 1-31)
- month: Last contact month of year (categorical: jan, feb, ..., dec)
- duration: Last contact duration in seconds (numeric)
- campaign: Number of contacts performed during this campaign (numeric)
- pdays: Number of days since client was last contacted from previous campaign (numeric)
- previous: Number of contacts performed before this campaign (numeric)
- poutcome: Outcome of previous marketing campaign (categorical: failure, nonexistent, success)
- y: Original target - has client subscribed to term deposit? (categorical: yes, no)
- contact_date: Synthesized date column in YYYY-MM-DD format (2008-05-01 to 2010-12-28)
- subscribed: Binary target derived from 'y' (numeric: 1=yes, 0=no)

Dataset Statistics:
- Total Instances: 45,211
- Number of Original Features: 16 + 1 target
- Number of Features (with additions): 17 + 2 derived columns
- Target Distribution: No (39,922 / 88.3%), Yes (5,289 / 11.7%)
- Date Range: May 2008 to December 2010
- Unique Dates: 394

Note on Data Modifications:
The 'contact_date' column in this version (bank_marketing_with_dates.csv) was synthesized
from the original 'month' and 'day_of_week' columns to create proper date values in
YYYY-MM-DD format. This modification was made to support date-type feature testing in
H2O Sonar's Partial Dependence/ICE explainer tests. The dates follow the original data
collection period (May 2008 - November 2010) as documented in the UCI repository.
