Description:
This dataset contains 380 observations with 9 variables from a prostate cancer study.
Variables measured at baseline patient examination were used to determine whether the
tumor had penetrated the prostate capsule. The observed values were modified from the
original data to protect patient confidentiality.

Variables:
- ID: Patient ID
- CAPSULE: Tumor penetration of prostatic capsule (0=no, 1=yes)
- AGE: Patient age in years
- RACE: Patient race (1=white, 2=black)
- DPROS: Results of digital rectal exam (1-4 scale)
- DCAPS: Detection of capsular involvement in rectal exam (0=no, 1=yes)
- PSA: Prostate Specific Antigen value (mg/ml)
- VOL: Tumor volume (from ultrasound, cc)
- GLEASON: Gleason score (total)
