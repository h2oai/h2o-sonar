Dataset: Bank Marketing Dataset
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository
URL: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/222/bank+marketing
DOI: 10.24432/C5K306

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

Description:
The data is related to direct marketing campaigns (phone calls) of a Portuguese banking
institution. The classification goal is to predict if the client will subscribe to a
term deposit (variable y). This is a sampled version of the dataset.
